About Us

Our History, Vision, and Mission

History & Foundation

Established in 1963 in Ragama, Diyagala Boys' Town was founded to fulfill a critical social need: providing practical education and livelihood skills to young boys from marginalized backgrounds.


Managed by the Lasallian Brothers (District of Colombo), the institute operates on the principle that every young person deserves access to quality education, proper care, and the opportunity to build a dignified livelihood regardless of their socioeconomic background.

Vision & Mission

👁️

Vision

To be a sanctuary of hope and excellence, transforming vulnerable youth into skilled professionals, leaders of character, and upright citizens.

🎯

Mission

To provide quality vocational, technical, and life-skills education enriched with Lasallian values, fostering discipline, self-reliance, and social responsibility in a supportive residential community.

🤝

The Lasallian Identity

Highlighting three central virtues: Faith (instilling moral integrity), Service (prioritizing the poor), and Community (fostering brotherhood and mutual respect).

Campus Facilities & Infrastructure

Technical Workshops

Fully equipped motor garage, electrical laboratory, and mechanical workshop.

Agricultural Lands

Practical training grounds for sustainable farming, crop cultivation, and animal husbandry.

Residential Facilities

Safe dormitories, dining halls, study areas, and expansive sports grounds.
