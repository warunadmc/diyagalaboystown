Welcome to Diyagala Boys' Town

Located in Ragama, Sri Lanka, Diyagala Boys' Town is a residential vocational training institute managed by the De La Salle Brothers of the Christian Schools.


For over six decades, our institute has provided underprivileged and deserving young men with holistic human and Christian education, technical skill development, and character building. We empower our students to break the cycle of poverty and enter society as skilled, responsible, and self-reliant citizens.
